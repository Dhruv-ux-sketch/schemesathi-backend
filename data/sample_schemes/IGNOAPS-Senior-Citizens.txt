Indira Gandhi National Old Age Pension Scheme (IGNOAPS)

Description:
IGNOAPS is a central government scheme under the National Social Assistance
Programme (NSAP) that provides monthly financial assistance to elderly
citizens living below the poverty line, helping them meet basic needs in the
absence of a regular income or family support.

Benefits:
Eligible beneficiaries receive a monthly pension. The central government
contributes Rs. 200 per month for beneficiaries aged 60-79, and Rs. 500 per
month for beneficiaries aged 80 and above. Many state governments supplement
this amount further from their own funds, so the actual amount received
often varies by state and can be significantly higher than the central
contribution alone.

Eligibility:
- The applicant must be 60 years of age or older.
- The applicant's family must belong to a household living Below the Poverty
  Line (BPL), as per criteria defined by the state government or the
  Socio-Economic Caste Census.
- The applicant should not be receiving any other government pension.

Required Documents:
- Age proof (Aadhaar card, birth certificate, or voter ID)
- BPL certificate or ration card indicating BPL status
- Bank account or post office account details for direct benefit transfer
- Passport-size photograph

Application Process:
1. Obtain the IGNOAPS application form from the local Gram Panchayat,
   Municipal office, or the Social Welfare Department.
2. Fill in the form and attach age proof, BPL certificate, and bank details.
3. Submit the form to the local revenue officer or Panchayat for
   verification.
4. Once approved, the pension amount is credited directly to the
   beneficiary's bank or post office account every month.
