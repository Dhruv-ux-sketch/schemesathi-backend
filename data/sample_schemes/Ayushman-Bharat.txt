Ayushman Bharat - Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (AB PM-JAY)

Description:
Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY is the world's largest health assurance scheme, aiming
to provide free access to health insurance coverage for economically
vulnerable people in India, covering secondary and tertiary care hospitalization.

Benefits:
Provides health cover of Rs. 5 lakh per family per year for secondary and
tertiary care hospitalization, cashless and paperless access to services at
the point of care, at any empanelled public or private hospital across India.

Eligibility:
Eligibility is primarily determined using the Socio-Economic Caste Census
(SECC) 2011 database for both rural and urban areas, covering deprived rural
families and identified occupational categories in urban areas. There is no
cap on family size or age. Beneficiaries can check their eligibility on the
official portal using their mobile number or ration card.

Required Documents:
- Aadhaar card
- Ration card or SECC verification
- Mobile number for OTP verification
- Any government-issued photo ID

Application Process:
1. Check eligibility on the PM-JAY website or via the Ayushman Bharat mobile app.
2. If eligible, visit a nearby empanelled hospital or Common Service Centre.
3. Complete e-KYC verification using Aadhaar.
4. Receive the Ayushman Card, which can be used for cashless treatment at
   empanelled hospitals.

Official website: https://beneficiary.nha.gov.in/
