Pradhan Mantri Mudra Yojana (PMMY)

Description:
PMMY provides collateral-free loans to non-corporate, non-farm small and
micro enterprises for income-generating activities in manufacturing, trading,
and services, including activities allied to agriculture. It aims to fund
the "unfunded" — small entrepreneurs who typically can't access formal
bank credit.

Benefits:
Loans are offered under three categories based on the growth stage of the
business:
- Shishu: loans up to Rs. 50,000 for very early-stage businesses.
- Kishor: loans from Rs. 50,001 to Rs. 5 lakh for growing businesses.
- Tarun: loans from Rs. 5,00,001 to Rs. 10 lakh for established businesses
  looking to expand.
No collateral is required for these loans, and there is no processing fee
for Shishu category loans.

Eligibility:
- Any Indian citizen with a business plan for a non-farm income-generating
  activity such as manufacturing, processing, trading, or services.
- Includes small manufacturing units, shopkeepers, fruit/vegetable vendors,
  artisans, food service units, and repair shops.
- The business should not be a corporate entity (i.e., proprietorships,
  partnerships, and small firms are eligible, not large private/public
  limited companies).

Required Documents:
- Duly filled Mudra loan application form
- Identity proof (Aadhaar, voter ID, PAN, passport, driving license)
- Address proof
- Business plan or proof of existing business (if applicable)
- Quotations of machinery/goods to be purchased (if applicable)
- Passport-size photographs

Application Process:
1. Approach any public sector bank, private bank, regional rural bank,
   cooperative bank, or microfinance institution.
2. Alternatively, apply online through the official Mudra portal or the
   Udyamimitra portal.
3. Submit the application along with required documents and business plan.
4. Upon approval, the loan is disbursed along with a Mudra Card, which can
   be used like a debit card for business expenses.

Official Website: https://www.mudra.org.in
