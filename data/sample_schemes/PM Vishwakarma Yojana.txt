Scheme Name: PM Vishwakarma Yojana

Category: Skill Development, Employment, Financial Assistance

Description:
PM Vishwakarma Yojana is a Central Government scheme launched to support traditional artisans and craftspeople. The scheme provides skill training, toolkit incentives, collateral-free loans, digital transaction incentives, and marketing support to improve livelihoods and promote self-employment.

Eligibility:
- Applicant must be an Indian citizen.
- Must be engaged in a traditional artisan or craft occupation.
- Must be at least 18 years of age.
- Should not have received similar government assistance under specified schemes during the eligibility period.

Benefits:
- Basic and advanced skill training.
- Toolkit incentive of ₹15,000.
- Collateral-free loan up to ₹3,00,000 in two phases.
- Subsidized interest rate on loans.
- Digital transaction incentives.
- Marketing and branding support.
- Certificate and ID card under PM Vishwakarma.

Required Documents:
- Aadhaar Card
- Mobile Number linked with Aadhaar
- Bank Account Details
- Address Proof
- Passport-size Photograph

Application Process:
1. Visit the official PM Vishwakarma portal.
2. Register using Aadhaar authentication.
3. Fill in the online application form.
4. Upload the required documents.
5. Submit the application.
6. Track the application status online.

Official website: https://pmvishwakarma.gov.in/
