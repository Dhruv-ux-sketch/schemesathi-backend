Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana (SSY)

Description:
Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana is a small savings scheme by the Government of India
specifically designed for the financial security of a girl child, encouraging
parents to build a fund for her education and marriage expenses.

Benefits:
The account earns a government-declared interest rate (reviewed quarterly,
historically among the highest of small savings schemes) and offers tax
benefits under Section 80C. The invested amount, accrued interest, and
maturity amount are all tax-exempt. Partial withdrawal (up to 50%) is allowed
once the girl turns 18, for education expenses.

Eligibility:
- The account can be opened by a parent or legal guardian in the name of a
  girl child from birth until she turns 10 years old.
- A family can open the account for a maximum of two girl children (with
  exceptions for twins/triplets).
- Minimum deposit of Rs. 250 per year and maximum of Rs. 1.5 lakh per year.
- The account matures 21 years from the date of opening, or upon the girl's
  marriage after she turns 18 (whichever is earlier).

Required Documents:
- Birth certificate of the girl child
- Identity proof and address proof of the parent/guardian (Aadhaar, PAN, etc.)
- Passport-size photograph of the girl child and guardian

Application Process:
1. Visit any authorized bank branch or post office.
2. Fill out the SSY account opening form.
3. Submit the required documents along with the initial deposit.
4. Continue making annual deposits (minimum Rs. 250) to keep the account active.
