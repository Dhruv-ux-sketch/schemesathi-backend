Atal Pension Yojana (APY)

Description:
Atal Pension Yojana is a government-backed pension scheme mainly targeted at
workers in the unorganized sector, such as daily wage laborers, domestic
workers, and gig/informal workers who don't have access to formal
employer-sponsored retirement plans.

Benefits:
Subscribers receive a guaranteed minimum monthly pension ranging from
Rs. 1,000 to Rs. 5,000 after the age of 60, depending on the contribution
amount and the age at which they join. The pension amount is fixed by the
subscriber at the time of enrollment, and the government guarantees this
minimum amount. In the event of the subscriber's death, the spouse receives
the same pension amount, and after both die, the nominee receives the
accumulated pension corpus.

Eligibility:
- Any Indian citizen between 18 and 40 years of age.
- Must have a savings bank account or post office savings account.
- Must not be an income taxpayer (this condition was added for enrollments
  from October 2022 onward).

Required Documents:
- Aadhaar card
- Active savings bank account or post office savings account passbook
- Mobile number linked to the bank account

Application Process:
1. Visit your bank branch or post office where you hold a savings account.
2. Fill out the APY registration form, selecting your desired monthly
   pension amount.
3. Provide your Aadhaar and mobile number for verification.
4. The chosen contribution amount is auto-debited from your bank account
   monthly, quarterly, or half-yearly until you turn 60.
