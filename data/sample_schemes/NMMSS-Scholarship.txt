National Means-cum-Merit Scholarship Scheme (NMMSS)

Description:
NMMSS is a central government scholarship scheme aimed at preventing dropout
at the class VIII to class XII level and encouraging meritorious students
from economically weaker sections to continue their education.

Benefits:
Selected students receive a scholarship of Rs. 12,000 per year (Rs. 1,000
per month) for four years, from class IX to class XII, subject to satisfactory
academic performance and attendance each year.

Eligibility:
- Student must be studying in class VIII in a government, government-aided,
  or local body school.
- Parental income from all sources must not exceed Rs. 3,50,000 per annum.
- Student must have secured a minimum of 55% marks (relaxable by 5% for
  SC/ST students) in the previous qualifying examination.
- Selection is through a state-level examination.

Required Documents:
- Income certificate of parents/guardian
- Caste certificate (if applicable)
- School certificate confirming enrollment in class VIII
- Bank account details of the student (Aadhaar-linked, for direct benefit transfer)
- Aadhaar card

Application Process:
1. Check the state education department notification for exam dates.
2. Register and appear for the NMMSS state-level selection test (MAT + SAT).
3. On qualifying, submit required documents through the school or the National
   Scholarship Portal (NSP).
4. Scholarship amount is disbursed directly into the student's bank account
   each year, subject to renewal conditions.
