PM-KISAN (Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi)

Description:
PM-KISAN is a central government scheme that provides income support to all
landholding farmer families in India to supplement their financial needs for
procuring inputs related to agriculture and allied activities.

Benefits:
Eligible farmer families receive Rs. 6,000 per year in three equal installments
of Rs. 2,000 each, transferred directly into their bank accounts every four months.

Eligibility:
All landholding farmer families, subject to certain exclusion criteria, are
eligible. Excluded categories include institutional landholders, farmer
families with one or more members who are current or former holders of
constitutional posts, current or former ministers, MPs, MLAs, mayors, or
serving/retired government employees (with some exceptions for Group D/Class
IV employees), and those who paid income tax in the last assessment year.

Required Documents:
- Aadhaar card
- Land ownership documents (Khatauni/Khasra)
- Bank account details (passbook)
- Passport-size photograph

Application Process:
1. Visit the official PM-KISAN portal or the nearest Common Service Centre (CSC).
2. Register using Aadhaar number and provide land and bank details.
3. Submit the application for verification by the local revenue/agriculture department.
4. Once approved, installments are credited directly to the registered bank account.
